What the analysis answers

From raw demand to a replenishment decision.

The pipeline is deliberately database-optional and fully reproducible. Each layer answers a different planning question and feeds the next.

01 · SEGMENT

Which SKUs matter most?

Value-based ABC classes meet calendar-complete demand variability (XYZ), exposing the high-value, hard-to-forecast items that deserve planner attention.

ABC-XYZ
02 · SET POLICY

How much should we hold?

EOQ sets the economic order quantity. Service-level safety stock and reorder points are calculated at 90%, 95%, and 99%, with the target policy applied per SKU.

EOQ · SS · ROP
03 · QUANTIFY RISK

What could go wrong?

ETS and Holt-Winters forecasts feed a 20,000-run lead-time demand simulation. The output is a stockout probability, revenue exposure, and a suggested action.

Forecast · Monte Carlo
